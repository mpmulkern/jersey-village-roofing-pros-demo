Why Preventive Maintenance Matters in Houston

  • Gulf Coast heat and UV exposure accelerate shingle and membrane aging
  • Annual checks catch loose flashing and minor leaks before storm season
  • Reflective coating can lower attic temperatures and reduce cooling costs
  • Regular maintenance is typically far cheaper than emergency repair

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a roof coating do?

A reflective roof coating reduces surface heat absorption, slows shingle or membrane aging, and adds a protective, water-resistant layer, which can extend roof life by several years in the Houston climate.

How often should I have my roof maintained?

Jersey Village Roofing Pros recommends a maintenance check once a year, and after any significant storm, to catch small issues like loose flashing or clogged valleys before they become leaks.

Is roof coating a substitute for replacement?

Coating is a preventive, cost-effective option for roofs still in reasonably good condition. A roof with structural damage or widespread wear needs repair or replacement instead, which we'll tell you plainly during a free inspection.
