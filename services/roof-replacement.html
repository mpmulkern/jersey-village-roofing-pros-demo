Signs You Need a Roof Replacement

  • Roof is 20+ years old (asphalt shingle) or showing widespread wear
  • Multiple repairs in the past 1–2 years
  • Visible sagging, granule loss, or daylight through the decking
  • Recent hail or wind storm with widespread shingle damage

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most residential roof replacements in Jersey Village and Houston take 1–3 days depending on roof size and material. Metal and tile roofs can take slightly longer.

How much does a new roof cost in Jersey Village?

A full asphalt shingle replacement typically runs $9,000–$18,000 depending on roof size and pitch; metal and tile run higher. We provide a free, itemized estimate before any work begins.

Will my roof replacement be covered by insurance?

If your roof was damaged by hail or wind, it may be covered. We provide documentation to support your insurance claim — see our storm damage restoration page for details.
