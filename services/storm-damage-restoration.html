Hail vs. Wind Damage

Hail Damage

Look for dented or bruised shingles, granule loss in gutters, and dented gutters/vents — often not visible from the ground.

Wind Damage

Lifted, creased, or missing shingles, exposed underlayment, and detached flashing, common during Gulf Coast storm season.

Quick Answer: What should I do right after storm damage?

Avoid getting on the roof yourself. Take photos from the ground if it's safe, and call a licensed roofing contractor for a free inspection before contacting your insurance company — documented damage strengthens your claim.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does homeowners insurance cover roof storm damage?

Most Texas homeowners policies cover sudden hail and wind damage, though coverage details vary. We provide a documented damage report to support your claim, and can meet your adjuster on-site.

How fast can you respond after a storm?

We prioritize emergency tarping same-day for active leaks, with a full inspection and estimate typically within 24–48 hours after a major storm.

Will filing a storm damage claim raise my insurance rates?

Weather-related claims (hail, wind) are generally treated differently from at-fault claims by most Texas insurers, but you should confirm specifics with your provider.
