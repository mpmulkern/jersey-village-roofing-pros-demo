Frequently Asked Questions

How much does roof repair cost in Jersey Village?

Most minor roof repairs (shingle replacement, small leaks, flashing) in Jersey Village and Houston run $300–$1,200. Larger repairs involving decking replacement can run higher. Jersey Village Roofing Pros provides a free written estimate before any work starts.

Can you repair a roof leak the same day?

In most cases yes. We prioritize active leaks and offer emergency tarping the same day for storm-related damage, with permanent repair scheduled within 1–3 days.

Do you repair all roof types?

Yes — asphalt shingle, metal, tile, and flat/low-slope roofing common on Jersey Village and Houston-area homes and light commercial buildings.
