What We See on Spring Roofs

Spring has a wider spread of roof ages than most of the metro, and the failure patterns follow. Established neighbourhoods with mature pine and oak cover get abrasion from overhanging limbs, needle build-up in valleys that holds moisture against the shingle mat, and gutters that clog faster than anywhere else we work. Newer Grand Parkway construction has the opposite profile: sound decking, but builder-grade three-tab shingles reaching the end of a short service life all at once across a whole street. Both are worth an inspection before a leak forces the decision.
