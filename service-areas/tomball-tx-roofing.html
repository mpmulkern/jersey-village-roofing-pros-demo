Storm Season in Northwest Harris County

Tomball sits in a part of the metro that reliably catches spring hail. The storm tracks that run down from Waller and Montgomery counties tend to arrive with little warning in March through May, and the damage they leave is often invisible from the driveway. Hail bruises the asphalt mat without cracking the surface granules, so the roof looks intact while its waterproofing life has been cut short by years. That damage typically surfaces as a leak one or two seasons later, by which point the claim window on the original storm may have closed.

If a hail event has passed over your part of Tomball, book an inspection while the date is still documented rather than waiting for water to appear on a ceiling. We photograph and measure impact marks against the storm date so the claim reflects what actually happened, and we will tell you plainly when a roof has not been damaged enough to justify filing.
