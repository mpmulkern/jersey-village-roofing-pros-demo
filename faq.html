How do I know if I need a roof repair or a full replacement?

If your roof is under 15 years old with isolated damage (a leak, a few missing shingles), repair is usually sufficient. If it's 20+ years old, has had multiple recent repairs, or shows widespread granule loss and sagging, replacement is typically more cost-effective long-term. A free inspection will give you a clear recommendation either way.

Will my roof repair or replacement be covered by insurance?

Damage caused by hail, wind, or other sudden storm events is often covered by homeowners insurance, subject to your policy terms. We document damage thoroughly and can meet with your insurance adjuster to support your claim. General wear and aging are typically not covered.
