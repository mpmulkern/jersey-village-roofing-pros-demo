Storm Damage Roof Insurance Claim Guide for Jersey Village, TX

Filing a roof storm damage insurance claim can feel confusing right after a hailstorm or high-wind event. This guide walks Jersey Village and Houston-metro homeowners through what to do first, how to document damage, and how Jersey Village Roofing Pros supports your claim from inspection to repair.

Quick Answer: What's the first step after storm damage?

Document visible damage from the ground with photos and video, then call a licensed roofing contractor for a free inspection and written report before you contact your insurer — never climb onto a storm-damaged roof yourself.

Step-by-Step: Filing a Storm Damage Roof Claim

  • 1. Document from the ground. Photograph visible shingle damage, dented gutters, and debris. Do not climb onto the roof.
  • 2. Get a professional inspection. Jersey Village Roofing Pros provides a free, written inspection report with photos, which becomes your evidence for the claim.
  • 3. Contact your insurer. File the claim promptly, referencing the storm date and your written inspection report.
  • 4. Meet the adjuster. We can be on-site when your adjuster inspects the roof, to walk through documented damage together.
  • 5. Schedule the repair. Once your claim is approved, we schedule repair or replacement, working directly from the approved scope.

What We Document During an Inspection

Shingle & Surface Damage

Hail bruising, cracked or missing shingles, and granule loss photographed and logged.

Flashing & Vents

Wind-lifted flashing and damaged vent boots documented as common leak sources after storms.

Gutters & Downspouts

Dented or detached gutters, a common visible sign of hail impact adjusters look for.

Interior Water Signs

Ceiling stains and attic moisture that support a claim tied to roof-level damage.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do first after storm damage to my roof?

Document the damage with photos and video from the ground first (never climb onto a damaged roof), then call a licensed roofing contractor for a free inspection and written damage report before contacting your insurer.

Will Jersey Village Roofing Pros meet with my insurance adjuster?

Yes. We can meet your insurance adjuster on-site, walk through the documented damage, and support your claim with our written inspection report and photos.

How long do I have to file a storm damage claim in Texas?

Texas policies vary, but most require filing within one year of the storm event. Check your specific policy, and don't wait — document damage and file promptly after any major storm.
